## Week-one review

The public timeline showed the launch post about one minute old at approximately 1:50 p.m. on November 23. Use about 1:49 p.m. local time as the pilot start. The first-week checkpoint is November 30. Record live links and any link-click and view totals provided by X. Keep platform numbers and site totals separate. Run the [aggregate query](../../scripts/promotion-pilot-metrics.sql) at launch for the baseline and after seven days using the exact launch time plus seven days as its endpoint. It reports sitewide accepted public questions, distinct submitting accounts, officials asked, featured-profile questions and questions with official answers. It excludes imported and seeded questions, guest sessions, test profiles, hidden questions and AI responses. The output contains no individual account records.

The first rehearsal of the query found one accepted question from one account in the recent seven-day window and zero in the preceding week. This pre-launch count is a baseline. A first-week target is five accepted questions from three distinct accounts. Record founder and QA activity separately from audience activity. On Day 7, review the counts, reports, signup or submission errors and the operational workload before expanding. Count an official answer only when it is published and linked to a public question.
