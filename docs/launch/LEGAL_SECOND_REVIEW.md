## Legal boundaries to retain

Federal employee work within official duties is generally outside US copyright, subject to statutory exceptions. Government-hosted, state/local and contractor photographs do not inherit that status automatically. A licensed editorial portrait does not itself authorize advertising endorsement. Record the exact work, creator, grant, required notices, modifications and identity match. [US government copyright guidance](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright), [17 U.S.C. 105](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/USCODE-2023-title17/html/USCODE-2023-title17-chap1-sec105.htm)

CC BY-SA can require adaptation licensing as well as supplied attribution; it does not automatically license the entire independent site or waive personality/endorsement rights. Photo replacement must preserve credit and relevant history. DMCA process addresses conditional safe-harbor requirements; it does not license WhyTho's own imports. [CC BY-SA 4.0 terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en), [Copyright Office DMCA guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca/)

The site's independent status does not alone decide whether an official's future moderation/blocking constitutes state action. Review actual authority and conduct before enabling official control over constituent discussion. Government agency records obligations can also require export/history support; they are not a blanket rule requiring WhyTho to keep every identifier forever. [Lindke v. Freed](https://www.supremecourt.gov/opinions/23pdf/22-611_ap6c.pdf), [NARA guidance](https://www.archives.gov/records-mgmt/bulletins/2014/2014-02.html)
