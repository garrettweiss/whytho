Effective November 23, 2026.

### About WhyTho

WhyTho is operated by Zellon LLC, a California limited liability company, at 2108 N St., Ste N, Sacramento, CA 95816. Contact [support@whytho.us](mailto:support@whytho.us) for help or questions about these terms.

WhyTho is an independent civic question-and-answer service. Public profiles may be created from public sources before an official or candidate joins. A listing does not imply endorsement or participation. We are not an election authority. Office, candidacy and other public-record information can change; please report errors.

### Participation and accounts

You must be at least 18 to participate, including creating an account, asking questions, voting, using interactive chat or managing an office profile. Public pages are available to read without an account. Confirm your eligibility and agreement when prompted; the age confirmation does not require a birth date. Use an account you control and complete the verification required for the feature. You may use a public display name. Email verification does not certify your legal identity, age or residence.

You may ask politicians questions outside your district. Location profile fields are optional and self-reported. They do not establish verified constituent status or give your vote extra weight. Political affiliation is not required to participate.

New votes require a registered account. Each account may hold one active upvote or downvote per question. During an open voting period, you may change direction or remove your vote. Earlier guest votes and submissions may remain in the record; the registration requirement does not retrospectively establish who made them. Do not use extra accounts or automation to manipulate totals. A count of accounts or votes is not proof that each represents a unique person or a local constituent.

### Politicians and office teams

Claim only profiles you are authorized to manage. A claim alone does not establish that authority. Verification applies to a specific profile and office relationship; it does not certify the truth of every response. Public responses require the applicable verified office status and authorized team role.

Authorized staff may answer for an office. Such responses are identified as “Answered by [politician]'s office” and are not represented as personally written by the politician. Keep team access current and report suspected account compromise promptly to [security@whytho.us](mailto:security@whytho.us).

An office transition does not transfer the predecessor's answer authorship or account access to the incoming officeholder. Former officials retain their historical profiles and dated answers. We may pause further publication while investigating a disputed claim or suspected compromise.

### Your content and permission

You retain the rights you hold in your submissions. By submitting content for publication, you grant Zellon LLC a nonexclusive, worldwide, royalty-free permission to host, reproduce, format, display and distribute it as part of operating WhyTho and maintaining the public civic record described below. We may use service providers for these purposes. This permission covers technical resizing or formatting; it does not authorize us to fabricate a statement or materially change what you said. It does not transfer copyright ownership to us.

For an answered question or an authentic official response, this permission continues for the public archive after account closure, subject to applicable law and the rights process below. Other content remains subject to that process and the relevant retention rules. You can grant only rights you actually hold; this provision does not replace a third party's license conditions.

Upload only media you have permission to publish. A portrait replacement requires authority to manage the profile and permission covering the image's display and technical processing. Supply accurate creator, credit and rights information when requested. A likeness must depict the right person. These permissions do not themselves authorize use of anyone's name, portrait or response as a product endorsement in advertising.

### The public answer archive

Authentic published answers from politicians or their authorized offices are intended to remain a permanent public civic record, including after account closure or departure from office. Published answer originals remain visible alongside dated corrections. Contact support about a disputed attribution, retraction or identifying information; these cases require review rather than a silent rewrite of the original answer.

An answered question and its official response do not automatically disappear when the asker's account closes. We review requests to detach the former account's public identity and to address private information in the text. A request from an authentic author to correct an answer will be shown with the correction they supply; we do not invent a correction or attribute a request that was not made.

This archive policy does not override applicable law. Fraudulent attribution, unlawful material, exposed private information and other valid rights concerns may require restrictions or withdrawal after review. Proven fraudulent answers are not treated as authentic official positions. Relevant restricted evidence and the reason for an action may be retained when justified.

### Suggestions, AI and response records

Optional suggestions help you develop a question. Choose and edit what you want to ask; a draft prepared for you in chat is not published by that action or evidence of public support. WhyTho may separately display questions from its research process. We use AI in these features, research and chat, and may use it to assist report review, as explained in the [privacy notice](/privacy). AI can make mistakes. AI analysis is separate from an official answer and must not be attributed to a politician or office.

The response rate measures qualifying questions with a published official response divided by qualifying questions in the displayed period. A qualifying question has at least ten net votes, meaning upvotes minus downvotes. Multiple responses to one question count as one answered question. Private drafts and AI analysis do not count as official responses. No rate is shown when there are no qualifying questions.

No recorded answer does not establish that an official received, read or refused a question. WhyTho does not guarantee an answer, certify a response as factually true, or measure the wider electorate's opinion through its voting totals.

### Conduct and complaints

Ask substantive questions and disagree freely about public issues. Do not impersonate people, threaten or harass others, expose private information, submit content you cannot lawfully share, manipulate votes, or bypass security controls. Do not present an unsupported factual accusation as established fact.

Users and nonusers may report errors, harmful material, impersonation or accessibility problems to [support@whytho.us](mailto:support@whytho.us). Send personal-data requests to [privacy@whytho.us](mailto:privacy@whytho.us), copyright complaints to [copyright@whytho.us](mailto:copyright@whytho.us), and vulnerabilities to [security@whytho.us](mailto:security@whytho.us). Include the relevant page address and enough information to understand the concern. Do not send another copy of illegal or intimate media.

Use the [intimate-image removal process](/report#intimate-images) to report an intimate image shared without consent, including a fabricated intimate image. You do not need a WhyTho account to use that process.

We may restrict content or account capabilities for documented safety, rights or integrity reasons. We consider the content and evidence, not the speaker's political party. A report count alone is not a finding of wrongdoing. We provide reasons when appropriate and accept requests for reconsideration through support. Privacy and legal obligations may limit the details we can share.

Our copyright policy provides for terminating accounts in appropriate circumstances for repeated infringement. We review the evidence and any correction or counter-notice; an accusation alone does not establish infringement.

### Email and account closure

Automated new-question delivery to offices is currently disabled. Saving an office notification preference does not mean delivery has begun. Registered users can manage available answer and digest preferences in account settings. Changing an email preference does not remove the public profile or its answer archive. Account-security messages are separate from optional alerts.

You can request account closure through [privacy@whytho.us](mailto:privacy@whytho.us). Office administrators should arrange an authorized transfer before leaving an active team. Access removal, private-data handling and requests concerning public content are distinct parts of closure; we will explain the relevant outcome.

### Availability and changes

We work to keep WhyTho available and accurate, but availability, third-party sources and responses can change. Use official election sources for voting deadlines, eligibility and procedures. These terms do not waive rights that applicable law does not permit you to waive.

We will identify the effective date of updated terms and provide appropriate notice of material changes. Where renewed agreement is required, we will request it. Contact support if you have a question about a change.
