## Routine and urgent workflow

1. **Receive and classify.** Garrett checks the original recipient and timestamp, deduplicates related reports and preserves the earliest relevant receipt. Urgent harm, suspected compromise, exposed private information and intimate-image notices take priority over the normal queue. Ordinary internal targets remain acknowledgement within two business days and substantive review within five; they are proposed targets, not legal extensions or public service promises.
2. **Prepare the decision.** Establish the actual content, author/identity, public/private status, relevant source evidence and possible affected copies. Distinguish an allegation from established facts. Preserve the original official-answer archive and dated corrections by default; a narrow legal, privacy or fraudulent-attribution exception needs an item-specific decision.
3. **Approve and act.** Garrett approves external replies, live changes, restrictions and the exact affected items. Actual deletion requires his explicit confirmation identifying what and why. Reversible access withdrawal can be prepared without deleting originals, but this procedure is not blanket authorization to execute it.
4. **Verify and close.** Check the actual public access path, alternate links, cache behavior and relevant roles. Record outcome, rationale and remaining exceptions. Send only the approved response. Offer reconsideration; do not claim an independent appeals team exists. A report count is a triage signal, not proof of misconduct.

Before accepting an urgent workload, Garrett needs a workable interruption/notification arrangement and a documented absence plan. Recommended operating checkpoints are immediate notification on discovery, an early decision, an internal completion target well before the legal deadline, and escalation as soon as a deadline is at risk. These are controls to arrange, not a claim that alerts are installed. No new Codex/mail automation was created by this document.

## Intimate-image notice and 48-hour procedure

Treat WhyTho's public question-and-answer forum as likely within the TAKE IT DOWN Act's covered-platform definition pending a fact-specific determination. Civic subject matter is not an exemption. The platform process has applied since May 19, 2026, and covers qualifying authentic imagery and digital forgeries. Publish a clear process usable by people without accounts. [FTC guidance](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/complying-take-it-down-act)

Collect in writing: the individual's or authorized representative's physical/electronic signature; information sufficient to identify and locate the depiction; a brief good-faith statement that publication was without consent with relevant facts; and usable contact details. Do not require another media upload. For a valid request, remove as soon as possible, no later than 48 hours after receipt, and make reasonable efforts to identify and remove known identical copies. [Public Law 119-12, section 3](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/PLAW-119publ12/html/PLAW-119publ12.htm)

Operational steps:

1. Record receipt immediately when discovered and calculate the deadline from the mailbox receipt, not the time someone opens it. Retain the original receipt even if clarification is needed; do not restart a valid request's clock through internal reassignment or an unnecessary verification demand.
2. Ask only for material missing information. A malformed notice can still reveal urgent harm requiring protective review. Preserve the notice separately from media, limit access and flag overlapping child-safety duties.
3. Identify the reported asset, linked answer/question/profile, storage object, rendered variants, direct URLs, embeds and known duplicates. Do not require the reporter to enumerate copies. Use existing metadata/hashes when available; record the reasonable search and its limitations. Avoid unnecessary viewing, downloading or redistribution.
4. Obtain Garrett's prompt item-specific decision and apply the approved access restriction. Escalate immediately when he is unavailable; rely on Quinn only after actual confirmation of availability and authority. The 48-hour clock continues over nights, weekends and holidays. Routine approval practices do not extend it.
5. Verify the page and the direct media path, all known copies within WhyTho's control, cache behavior and previous signed-link expiry. Record exact times and pending externally controlled copies. Send a factual update only after approval; do not claim universal internet erasure.
6. Do not apply DMCA counter-notice waiting periods to this request. Reinstatement needs a separate documented review, particularly where child-safety, privacy or other independent restrictions overlap.

**Completed clock exercise:** a synthetic valid request received Friday November 27 at 23:30 in `America/Bahia_Banderas` (UTC−06:00) has an outer deadline Sunday November 29 at 23:30 in that zone, equivalently November 30 at 05:30 UTC. The calculation was run locally with timezone-aware datetime arithmetic. Waiting for Monday morning fails. This is a fictional exercise, not a received report.

### Reversible answer-attachment quarantine

The implementation team is adding `answer_media.access_disabled_at`, private read restrictions and a signing-route guard, plus removal of attachment imagery from newly generated share cards. **Treat this as pending until the migration, route and denial tests are confirmed deployed.** The following is a prepared operator procedure, not an executed command.

1. Confirm the environment and deployed quarantine column/guards. Match the exact media ID to the reported URL and its answer; record the current state in the restricted case. Use authorized Supabase operator access, never paste service keys or privileged links into a report.
2. Obtain Garrett's approval for that case and exact media ID. In Supabase SQL Editor, replace the deliberately invalid placeholder below with the reviewed UUID. Do not execute broad predicates or disable controls to make a query work.

```sql
SELECT id, answer_id, politician_id, storage_path, access_disabled_at
FROM public.answer_media
WHERE id = '<reviewed-media-uuid>'::uuid;

UPDATE public.answer_media
SET access_disabled_at = now()
WHERE id = '<reviewed-media-uuid>'::uuid
  AND access_disabled_at IS NULL
RETURNING id;
```

3. Record the returned ID and timestamp. Zero returned rows means no new change, not proof of success: distinguish an already quarantined row from a wrong/missing ID with the read query. The SQL preserves the record, object and public-answer history.
4. Verify fresh `/api/answer-media/[id]` access is denied for logged-out, unrelated, responder, editor and admin clients. Verify direct public database reads cannot recover a usable path or bypass signing. Check an already-issued signed URL again after its recorded expiry of up to 60 seconds. A route change cannot retroactively revoke an already issued token.
5. Review known duplicates individually and quarantine each approved ID; check page previews, copied external links and historic share-card caches. No global purge or cache expiry was established by this exercise. Changing future share-card output alone does not clear old cached images. Public portraits and third-party hosts are outside this column's scope.
6. Restore only after a separate approved disposition and access test. Do not reset the flag automatically on appeal, replacement or a copyright counter-notice where another valid restriction remains.

## Privacy, copyright and incident decisions

**Privacy:** record the request and residence/jurisdiction facts needed to select an applicable rule; do not infer that every CCPA duty applies simply because Zellon is Californian. Where CCPA request rules apply, receipt confirmation is due within 10 business days and a response to access/correction/deletion within 45 calendar days, with a necessary extension and notice up to 90 total. Verification time does not reset receipt. These are distinct from urgent exposed-information handling. [CPPA regulations effective January 1, 2026, section 7021](https://www.cppa.ca.gov/regulations/pdf/ccpa_statute_eff_20260101.pdf)

Verify proportionately through the existing account or relevant authority. Prepare an itemized export/correction/closure package covering database, storage, processors and backups. Include optional city/county/state and any legacy affiliation still stored; stopping new affiliation collection does not erase old records or exclude them from a rights request. Preserve public authentic answers by default while assessing specific lawful exceptions; detaching an account does not anonymize words or media. Exclude other voters' identities and internal security evidence from exports. No self-service closure/export route or tested cross-vendor fulfilment was established in this review. Follow the [retention procedure](OUTREACH_AND_RETENTION_DRAFT.md); no automatic purge is authorized.

**Copyright:** collect the work, challenged material/location, claimant contact, signature, good-faith authorization statement and accuracy/authority statement under penalty of perjury. Substantially compliant notices need prompt assessment and expeditious action for the relevant safe-harbor process. A valid counter-notice has its own signature, identification, sworn mistake/misidentification statement and jurisdiction/service requirements. The statutory restoration window is generally 10 to 14 business days after receiving a valid counter-notice unless notified of the required court action; this is not the intimate-image deadline or automatic authority to restore independently restricted content. [Copyright Office section 512 overview](https://www.copyright.gov/512/), [17 U.S.C. 512(g)](https://www.copyright.gov/title17/92chap5.html#512)
