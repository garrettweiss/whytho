# Portrait rights and quality policy

Updated November 22, 2026. Engineering procedure reflecting Garrett's November 19 decision: perform permission checks as part of sourcing, without creating a routine manual approval queue for Garrett. It does not guarantee an image has no legal risk or claim attorney approval.

## Rights evidence before publication

For every photo record, retain exact subject ID, source file URL, source page, original creator, license identifier/version/URL or documented permission/public-domain basis, supplied notices, review date/reviewer, identity-match evidence and modifications. A profile name, Wikidata P18 URL, OpenStates record or .gov address alone is insufficient.

Permitted paths after the evidence check:

- Documented federal employee work produced in official duties, with author/agency context. A federal site may also host private/contractor works.
- CC0, CC BY or CC BY-SA with exact version and compliance with the file's notices. For adaptations, honor applicable ShareAlike on the image. Do not infer whole-site licensing obligations merely from an embedded image.
- Written permission covering WhyTho's intended storage, cropping, public directory and profile display.
- Verified profile admin upload with the recorded rights statement, named rights holder and an accurate likeness. Attestation is evidence of a representation, not proof of ownership; complaints still require review.

No scraped news photos, Google thumbnail URLs, campaign portraits or state/local-government photos without specific clearance. No invented photos of real politicians. No face-retouching that changes identity. No use in endorsement-style advertising without separate authorization/legal review.

## Legacy assets

Initial live inventory: 7,615 photo URLs among 11,513 active records, 5,784 mirrored and no populated original source field. Preserve assets and records for audit. Prioritize the launch cohort, federal incumbents, governors, and the sources with unclear commercial rights. Reconstruct provenance using exact original upstream identifiers/source records; do not assign a license based on visual similarity alone.

Unknown/conflicting rights use the already-approved neutral placeholder policy, preserving originals and history. Do not place routine batch-approval tasks on Garrett. Automated checks must reject missing/contradictory evidence and identify the reason. The October inventory above is historical; the November 22 congressional inventory and current photo-readiness report own the latest coverage evidence. No deletion or deployed mass replacement is authorized by this document. A coverage percentage is not rights clearance.

## Quality acceptance

Usable portrait target: clear current likeness, sufficient contrast, correct orientation, face visible, no watermark removed, no misleading crop, no advertising logo added. New uploads must be still JPEG/PNG/WebP, at most 4 MiB, at least 400 × 500 pixels, and within the pixel decode limit. Normalize to 400 × 500 JPEG, strip EXIF/location data, retain original audit identity and publish a new unique object path. Never upscale a tiny source and call it high quality.

Automated checks can establish reachability, dimensions, basic crop/quality signals, exact source identifiers and recorded license requirements. They cannot infer legal permission from a face match or prove current likeness solely from dimensions. Do not claim visual/editorial inspection was performed where it was not. Ambiguous identity or unreadable quality receives a placeholder or replacement from a better-supported source. Handle specific complaints individually; there is no standing manual approval queue assigned to Garrett.

## Display and replacement

Show creator/rights information through a nearby accessible credit or image-credit link, including source/license and modifications for licensed imports. Current upload UI displays named credit and the fact it was supplied by the verified team. Its permission covers profile/directory use, not implied product endorsement.

Only a verified team admin can replace a portrait. Preview the center crop before saving, validate on server, recheck membership under database lock at publication, and retain old/new URLs, actor, credit, exact rights version and time. Removed staff and another profile's admins cannot publish. Unauthorized attempts must not affect the public image. Rate limit uploads. Retained failed-upload objects are an operational reconciliation queue, not automatically deleted.

For a credible rights/identity complaint, preserve evidence, pause disputed display where appropriate, notify the responsible owner, and use the published correction/rights workflow. Restore only after documented review. Test recovery to a prior approved image without deleting history.

Sources: [USAgov](https://www.usa.gov/government-copyright), [Commons reuse guidance](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/Commons:Reusing_content_outside_Wikimedia), [CC BY-SA 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en), [Copyright Office](https://www.copyright.gov/dmca/).

USAgov and the CC BY-SA legal code were rechecked November 22, 2026. They continue to support per-work rights evidence, supplied credits/license notices, modification handling and separate endorsement/personality review. An official website alone is not a clearance certificate. These are continuing limits on the automated sourcing design, not an instruction to seek routine manual permission when an applicable license already authorizes the intended use.
