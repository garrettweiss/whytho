# State legislature member photos: reuse terms by state (checked 2026-11-25)

Method: fetched each legislature website's terms / copyright / disclaimer / privacy pages (WebFetch, and the Claude browser pane where WebFetch was blocked). Classification follows only what the pages (or the cited Commons template's legal basis) say. A bare footer notice ("© 2026 Name") is recorded as COPYRIGHT_RESERVED with "footer notice only" in Notes so it can be told apart from an explicit policy. No permission is inferred from a photo merely being displayed. Member-photo URL patterns were recorded only where encountered; no images were downloaded.

**REUSE_PERMITTED:** New York (Senate), Oklahoma (House), Utah.

Important caveats on the Commons-template leads the task mentioned:
- **California (PD-CAGov):** template basis is the CPRA (Gov. Code 7920.530) plus County of Santa Clara v. CFAC. But Gov. Code 7920.540(a) defines "state agency" as "except those agencies provided for in Article IV (except Section 20 thereof)" of the Constitution, i.e. the Legislature is outside the CPRA. The Assembly's own conditions-of-use page says permission must be obtained in advance. Classified COPYRIGHT_RESERVED on the site's words; the PD basis for legislator photos is not established by the pages read.
- **Massachusetts (PD-MAGov):** template basis is Secretary of the Commonwealth guidance, which the template itself says is "not definitive in the way a statute or a court ruling is". The General Court's Terms and Conditions expressly forbid downloading or reproducing any photograph without written consent. Classified COPYRIGHT_RESERVED.
- **Georgia (PD-GAGov):** template is marked factually disputed and nominated for deletion; the site footer asserts copyright. Classified COPYRIGHT_RESERVED (footer notice only).
- **Oklahoma (PD-OK-LSBPD):** template cites only a Wikimedia VRT permission ticket (#2013040810011067), no statute. The LSB Photography site footer reads "© 2026 LSB Photography. All rights reserved." The House site separately permits copying of posted information under conditions (REUSE_PERMITTED, House).
- **Florida (PD-FLGov):** basis is Fla. Const. Art. I s.24, s.119.011(12) F.S., and Microdecisions v. Skinner, 889 So. 2d 871 (Fla. 2d DCA 2004). Senate privacy page itself says all state records are public records. Classified PUBLIC_DOMAIN. Florida House site blocked all fetches.
- **North Carolina (PD-NCGov-legislator photo):** basis is ncleg.gov/Disclaimer. Both ncleg.gov hosts are Cloudflare-blocked from this environment; the disclaimer text was obtained only via a search-engine snippet. The statewide nc.gov Terms say use of photos on "any state website is strictly prohibited", which conflicts; the legislature's own disclaimer is the more specific statement.
