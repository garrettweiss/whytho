## Objective and operating assumptions

Get real people to publish specific, answerable questions and return to read official responses. The first month should prove the ask-to-answer loop. Use the same invitation and editorial rules for all officials. WhyTho is an independent civic platform, not an official government service or candidate endorsement.

Planning assumptions, not verified forecasts: two founders, 3 hours of content work per week, no paid spend, one brand account per channel, no existing audience size assumed. Pilot target: 100 accepted user questions from 50 distinct submitters across 10 profiles, with 5 published verified human answers by day 30. These are learning targets. Failure to get official answers is reported honestly; it does not turn AI output into a politician response.

Start with existing voluntarily engaged followers and people who choose to browse civic information. Invite participation through general platform education. Do not use political affiliation, inferred ideology, religion, race, age, private voter files or microtargeted voter segments. Location is a user-controlled directory choice, not a persuasion profile. Do not buy lists or pay for votes, comments, questions, followers or endorsements.

## Conversion journey

1. A post offers one clear action: “Find your representative and ask one specific question.” Link to [WhyTho](https://whytho.us/) or an accurately identified profile. Do not point traffic to a nonexistent “personalized feed.”
2. Visitor chooses a name/state/office. Explain that the site covers officials and candidates; verify office freshness before a featured profile is used.
3. Visitor reads existing questions. If an identical concern already exists, invite an upvote without manufacturing urgency or promising an answer.
4. Visitor submits their own question. Never submit an AI suggestion under the visitor's identity automatically.
5. Confirmation offers sharing and notification settings where implemented. Avoid saying the office saw a question because it was posted or an email was accepted by a provider.
6. When an official answer exists, link to the complete answer and preserve its format, date and attribution. When none exists, say “No official answer is recorded on WhyTho as of [timestamp].” Do not say “ignored,” “refused,” or “afraid to answer.”

All product claims must match the build used in the demo. Use test profiles clearly marked “demo” for screen recordings; no fabricated launch activity or composite testimonials.

**A4: Founder note, LinkedIn.** “We're building WhyTho to make public questions easier to find and official answers easier to follow. ## Measurement and decisions

| Metric | Exact definition | Initial target / decision use |
|---|---|---|
| Accepted user questions | Non-test, non-seeded, source `user`, published and not moderation-hidden, created during pilot | 100 by day 30 |
| Genuine submitters | Distinct non-test submitter IDs on accepted user questions; count anonymous sessions separately | 50 by day 30; do not call anonymous IDs verified people |
| Profile spread | Distinct canonical politician IDs receiving accepted user questions | 10; not a partisan quota |
| Official answers | Published `direct`/`team_statement`, human, correct politician, verified authorization; distinct answered questions also reported | 5 answered questions by day 30 |
| Return contribution | Of first-week submitters, count those asking/upvoting again in days 8-14, internally aggregated | Learn whether value persists; no benchmark claimed |
| Abuse/quality | Reports and human-reviewed violations per 100 new questions; duplicate-question share | Any credible doxxing/impersonation gets immediate review; rising spam pauses acquisition |
| Link effectiveness | Native-platform link clicks / impressions for the same post | Compare asset types; do not infer onsite conversion attribution while analytics are disabled |
| Reliability | Question publication error rate and team answer failure rate | Pause posts if a core path breaks |

Browser product analytics are currently paused by this launch work. Until a reviewed consent setup exists, use native-platform aggregates and database aggregates without exporting names, emails, question bodies or political-interest histories. Do not claim per-channel signup attribution without instrumentation. Future campaign tags may identify the asset only, never a person or ideology. Review retention and allowed analytics properties before enabling tracking.

At day 7: fix the largest submission friction before increasing volume. At day 14: if posts receive clicks but questions stay low, improve the ask flow and explanation, not the targeting. At day 30: expand only if no P0 issue remains, moderation is manageable, people return, and the answer workflow works. An official-response shortfall calls for opt-in office onboarding and better inbox usability; it does not authorize automated unsolicited contact.
